## Overview

TridentStack is an endpoint management platform focused on patch, vulnerability, and compliance management across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Key Features

- Patch management for Windows, macOS, and Linux endpoints
- Vulnerability management
- Compliance management
- Endpoint visibility and reporting

## Pricing

Free forever for up to 200 endpoints, with every feature included and no credit card required.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS platform.
