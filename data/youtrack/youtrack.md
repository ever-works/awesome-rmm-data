## Overview

YouTrack is an issue tracking and agile project management tool from JetBrains, with free hosting for FOSS and small private projects.

## Features

- Issue tracking
- Agile boards
- Time tracking
- Free hosted YouTrack (InCloud)
- Free for FOSS projects and private projects (up to 3 users)

## Pricing

Free for FOSS projects and up to 3 users for private projects. Paid plans for larger teams.
