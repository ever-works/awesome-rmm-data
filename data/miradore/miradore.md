## Overview

Miradore is a device management service for keeping device fleets up to date and secure, suitable for IT teams managing mixed device environments.

## Key Features

- Device fleet inventory and monitoring
- Device security and compliance
- Unlimited devices on the free plan
- Basic features included in the free tier

## Pricing

Freemium model: the free plan supports an unlimited number of devices with basic features. Paid plans add advanced capabilities.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS platform.
