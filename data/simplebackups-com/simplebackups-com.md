## Overview

SimpleBackups is a backup automation service for servers and databases, storing backups directly into cloud storage providers.

## Features

- Backup automation for servers and databases
- Supports MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB
- Stores backups into cloud storage providers including AWS, DigitalOcean, and Backblaze
- Free plan includes 1 backup

## Pricing

Freemium: a free plan for 1 backup, with paid tiers for additional backups and features.
