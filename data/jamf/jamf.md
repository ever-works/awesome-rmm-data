## Overview

Jamf is a device management platform focused on Apple devices, supporting iPads, iPhones, and Macs for enterprise and education use cases.

## Key Features

- Device management for iPad, iPhone, and Mac
- MDM enrollment and configuration
- Apple device fleet management

## Pricing

A free tier is available for up to three devices. Paid plans scale to larger device fleets.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS platform.
