## Overview

Basecamp is a project management and team communication tool featuring to-do lists, milestone management, messaging, file sharing, and time tracking.

## Features

- To-do lists
- Milestone management
- Forum-like messaging
- File sharing
- Time tracking
- Free plan: 3 projects, 20 users, 1GB storage

## Pricing

Free plan supports up to 3 projects, 20 users, and 1GB storage. Paid plans available.
