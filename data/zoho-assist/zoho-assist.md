## Overview

Zoho Assist is a cloud-based remote support and remote access software by Zoho Corporation. It allows technicians to provide remote support to customers and access unattended computers.

## Features

- Forever free plan with one concurrent remote support license
- Access to 5 unattended computer licenses for unlimited duration
- Suitable for both professional and personal use
- Integrates with Zoho Desk for one remote technician and 5 unattended computers
- Remote support and remote access capabilities

## Pricing

Free tier available (forever free plan). Paid plans offer additional features and licenses.
