## Overview

Toggl provides productivity tools including Toggl Track (time tracking) and Toggl Plan (task planning), both with free plans.

## Features

- Toggl Track: time management and tracking app
- Unlimited tracking records, projects, clients, tags, reporting
- Toggl Plan: task planning for solo developers
- Unlimited tasks, milestones, and timelines on free plan

## Pricing

Free plans available for both Toggl Track and Toggl Plan. Paid plans for team features.
