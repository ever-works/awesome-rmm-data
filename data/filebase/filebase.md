## Overview

Filebase is S3-compatible object storage powered by blockchain, providing geo-redundant storage across decentralized networks including IPFS, Filecoin, and Storj.

## Features

- S3-compatible API for integration with backup and storage tools
- Geo-redundant storage across IPFS, Filecoin, and Storj
- 5 GB free storage for unlimited duration
- Built-in redundancy and high availability
- Suitable for backup, archival, and content distribution

## Pricing Model

Free tier includes 5 GB of storage with unlimited duration. Additional storage and bandwidth are available on paid plans.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted service.
