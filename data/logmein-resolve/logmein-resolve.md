## Overview

LogMeIn Resolve is a remote support and remote access solution designed for IT support teams and managed service providers to troubleshoot and manage endpoints remotely.

## Features

- Unattended remote access
- On-demand remote support
- Multi-platform support (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android)
- Session diagnostics and management
- File transfer
- Remote reboot and reconnect
- Screen sharing and remote control

## Pricing

Paid subscription service with tiered pricing based on number of technicians and devices.
