## Overview

LogMeIn Hamachi is a hosted VPN service that enables secure extension of LAN-like networks to distributed teams.

## Features

- Hosted VPN service
- Securely extend LAN-like networks
- Free plan: unlimited networks with up to 5 people
- Distributed team support

## Pricing

Free plan available with unlimited networks (up to 5 people per network). Paid plans available for larger teams.
