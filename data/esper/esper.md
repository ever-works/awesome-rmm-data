## Overview

Esper is a mobile device management (MDM) and mobile application management (MAM) platform built for Android devices, combining device management with DevOps workflows.

## Key Features

- Android device provisioning and configuration
- Mobile application management (MAM)
- Device fleet management and monitoring
- DevOps-style device operations

## Pricing

The free plan includes support for up to 100 devices, one user license, and 25 MB of application storage. Paid plans are available for larger deployments.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS platform.
