## Overview

Leiga is an AI-powered SaaS project management tool that automates project management.

## Features

- AI-powered project management
- Free for up to 10 users
- 20 custom fields, 2GB storage
- Video recording with AI (5 mins/video limit)
- Automation runs (20/user/month)

## Pricing

Free plan available with limits on users, storage, and AI features. Paid plans for larger teams.
