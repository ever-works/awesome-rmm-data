## Overview

Puter is a web-based operating system designed to be feature-rich, exceptionally fast, and highly extensible, accessible through a web browser.

## Features

- Web-based operating system
- Feature-rich and fast
- Highly extensible
- Browser-based access
- Written in Node.js
- Docker support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker.
