## Overview

Onloc is a location tracking and sharing platform that enables real-time location sharing and remote control/locking of stolen or lost phones.

## Features

- Real-time location tracking
- Location sharing
- Remote phone control and locking
- Anti-theft features
- Docker support
- Mobile clients

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker.
