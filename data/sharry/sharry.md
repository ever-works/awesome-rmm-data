## Overview

Sharry is a browser-based screen sharing tool that requires no downloads or installations. It is useful for pair programming, presentations, or remote support scenarios.

## Features

- Screen sharing via browser with no downloads or installs
- No account required for free tier
- 5-minute session limit on free tier
- 1 viewer limit on free tier
- Suitable for pair programming, presentations, and remote support

## Pricing

Free tier available with limitations (5-minute sessions, 1 viewer, no account required). Additional details on paid plans available at the official website.
