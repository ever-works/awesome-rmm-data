## Overview

Tailscale is a zero-config VPN that uses the open-source WireGuard protocol to create secure private networks between devices.

## Features

- Zero-configuration setup
- WireGuard protocol
- Cross-platform support: macOS, iOS, Windows, Linux, Android
- Free plan for personal use
- 100 devices and 3 users on free plan

## Pricing

Free for personal use (up to 100 devices, 3 users). Paid plans available for teams and commercial use.
