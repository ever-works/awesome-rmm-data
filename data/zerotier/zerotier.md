## Overview

ZeroTier is a free and open-source managed virtual Ethernet service that creates end-to-end encrypted networks between devices.

## Features

- FOSS managed virtual Ethernet as a service
- Unlimited end-to-end encrypted networks
- Free plan: up to 25 clients
- Clients for desktop, mobile, and NAS
- Web interface for custom routing rules
- Client node approval on private networks

## Pricing

Free plan includes unlimited networks with up to 25 clients. Paid plans available for larger deployments.
