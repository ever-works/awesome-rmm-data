## Overview

VPS Snaps is an automated server backup and snapshots service spanning multiple cloud providers.

## Features

- Automated server backups and snapshots across 8 cloud providers
- Stores backups in your own bucket or Google Drive
- Free plan: 1 server, daily backups, no credit card required

## Pricing

Freemium: a free plan covering 1 server with daily backups (no credit card), with paid tiers for additional servers and features.
